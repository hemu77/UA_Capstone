03 / Interpretation

What held up? What didn't?

Friends of friends
Classical generators stayed competitive on clustering. The ordering changed with network size; the leading two differed by just 0.0037 on Facebook at 40 nodes.
Demographic mixing
Political orientation ranked first in all 28 conditions for each person-level method on two scores. For whole-network generation it led in six conditions on one score and only one on the other. One fictional roster and fixed prompt order cannot separate attribute effects.
Language contrast
Only one planned contrast survived adjustment: iterative Hindi vs English clustering was 0.045 lower. Its interval was −0.085 to −0.006; it survived only three of eight checks that removed one seed block. This is not a claim about Hindi speakers.

The 890 valid networks include 12 with no ties. Six later supplemental successes are separate and excluded throughout. This explorer shows final graphs, not a reconstruction of who chose whom first.
